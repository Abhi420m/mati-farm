The 4-Stage Provenance

How Every Mati Farm Vessel Is Created

We employ zero synthetic plasticizers, heavy-metal glazes, or petroleum lacquers. Sourced from organic riverbed clay and baked with sustainable wood-ash biomass.

01
Alluvial Riverbed Clay Extraction

Harvested from mineral-rich river deltas and triple-sieved through micro-mesh to achieve stone-free consistency.

02
Ergonomic Wheel Throwing

Hand-thrown on traditional flywheels by master artisans to ensure calibrated wall thickness and structural balance.

03
48-Hour Natural Solar Curing

Carefully dried under ambient sunlight to release internal moisture without inducing micro-fractures.

04
920°C Thermal Kiln Vitrification

Fired in traditional wood-and-straw kilns to solidify density and lock in the signature petrichor aroma.
